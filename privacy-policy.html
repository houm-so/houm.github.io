Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Houm ("Houm", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use the Houm website, mobile applications and related services (together, the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

This page is a general-purpose privacy policy template for the Houm app and is provided as a starting point. It should be reviewed by Houm's legal team before being treated as a final, binding policy.

1. Information we collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and password when you register.
  • Profile & identity information: details you provide as a property owner, tenant, buyer or agent, such as identity documents required to list, rent or purchase a property.
  • Property & listing information: addresses, photos, pricing and other details submitted when listing or searching for a property.
  • Payment information: billing details needed to process rent payments, deposits or fees, handled through our payment processors.
  • Usage & device information: app interactions, log data, device identifiers, IP address, and approximate location, collected automatically to operate and improve the App.
  • Communications: messages you send through the App, support requests, and correspondence with our team.

2. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account, and connect owners with tenants and buyers;
  • Facilitate listings, visits, digital contracts, payments and rent collection;
  • Verify identity and prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents;
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries;
  • Send service updates, transactional notices and, where permitted, marketing communications;
  • Analyze usage to improve the App's features, performance and pricing recommendations;
  • Comply with legal, tax and regulatory obligations in the countries where we operate.

3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Other users as needed to complete a transaction (for example, sharing a tenant's application with a property owner);
  • Service providers who help us operate the App, such as payment processors, identity verification providers, cloud hosting and analytics providers;
  • Professional advisors and authorities, where required to comply with the law or to protect our rights and the safety of our users;
  • An acquirer or successor in the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy or a substantially similar one.

4. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide the App, complete the transactions you request, and comply with our legal and tax obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

5. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can update most account information directly in the App, or contact us using the details below to exercise these rights.

6. Security

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. International data transfers

Houm operates across multiple countries in Latin America. Your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in a country other than the one in which you live, in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of majority in their local jurisdiction.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be communicated through the App or by other reasonable means.

10. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at privacy@houm.com.

Everything you need, in one app

From listing a property to signing a contract, Houm brings the whole process online.

🏠

List & publish

Owners can list a property for rent or sale in minutes, with photos, pricing and availability managed from the app.

🔍

Search & discover

Tenants and buyers browse verified listings, filter by neighborhood and price, and schedule visits directly in-app.

📝

Digital contracts

Sign rental agreements and manage paperwork online, without the back-and-forth of a traditional broker.

💳

Payments & collection

Rent collection and payment tracking are handled digitally, so owners get paid on time, every time.

🛠️

Property management

Tenant selection, maintenance coordination and day-to-day management are handled on the owner's behalf.

📈

Market data

Owners get data-driven pricing recommendations so properties rent or sell at the right price, faster.

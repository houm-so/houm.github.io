The homelab, in your pocket

One app for the smart home and the self‑hosted stack behind it

Houm is a modular app for iOS and Android that brings together the smart home your phone already belongs to and the self-hosted services that request, find, fetch and serve your media — all in one place, all switched on only if you run them.

See what's inside Read our Privacy Policy

Every service, in one place

Houm is built out of modules — connect the ones you run, and the app becomes exactly that.

🏠

Devices

Control the smart home your phone already belongs to — Apple Home on iOS, Google Home on Android — and watch a camera's live feed straight from the app.

🎬

Requests

Discover trending movies and shows, request what's missing, and track it through approval — signed in with your Plex account.

📚

Radarr & Sonarr

See your movie and TV library, what's queued and incoming, and search for anything still missing.

📥

NZBGet

Monitor and control your download manager — queue, speed, history and disk space, from your pocket.

▶️

Plex & Tautulli

See what's playing right now, who's watching, and stop a stream — plus a home-screen widget that keeps it one glance away.

🖥️

Proxmox, Unraid & Portainer

Watch the servers, storage and containers everything else runs on, and act on them without opening a browser.

Switch on what you run

Every service is a module you turn on or off — what's on decides what the app looks like.
